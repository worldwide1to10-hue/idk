## Deterministic Split-Gate Conditions

### Wide Execution (multi-agent DAG via `Task`)
All of the following must hold simultaneously:

| Condition | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Step threshold** | Estimated total steps **> 8** (more than eight) |
| **Sequential dependency** | **None** — later work must not depend on earlier discoveries or shared state (`sequential_dependency = false`) |
| **Independent branches** | **≥ 3** independently useful branches (capped at 8), and the work must be read-heavy or have disjoint writes (`read_heavy = true` and/or `disjoint_writes = true`) |

Each branch declares its exact `read_files`/`write_files` scope, dependencies (`needs`), budget, and acceptance checks.

### Single-Agent Execution (default)
Used whenever any wide-execution condition fails:
- ≤ 8 estimated steps, **or**
- sequential dependency exists, **or**
- fewer than three independent branches.

In that case, the work is completed directly without spawning sub-tasks.

---

For this specific request — simply stating the gate conditions — it is a single, non-sequential step well under the threshold, so it is completed directly above rather than via `Task`.
