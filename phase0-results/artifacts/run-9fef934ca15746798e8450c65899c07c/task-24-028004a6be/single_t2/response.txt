**Puzzle 1 – Sequential Reasoning**

**Initial state**  
- Queued jobs: **48**

**Step‑by‑step operations**

| Step | Operation | Result |
|------|-----------|--------|
| 1 | Complete **one third** of the jobs | \( \frac{1}{3} \times 48 = 16\) jobs finished<br>Remaining: \(48 - 16 = 32\) |
| 2 | **Receive 7 more** jobs | \(32 + 7 = 39\) jobs now in the queue |
| 3 | **Complete 9** jobs | \(39 - 9 = 30\) jobs left |

**Final remaining job count**: **30** jobs.
