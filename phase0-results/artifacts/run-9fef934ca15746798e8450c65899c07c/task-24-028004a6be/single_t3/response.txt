# Puzzle 1 Solution

**Puzzle:** A service starts with 48 queued jobs. It completes one third, then receives 7 more, then completes 9. How many remain?

## Sequence of Operations

| Step | Operation | Calculation | Jobs Remaining |
|------|-----------|-------------|----------------|
| 1 | Start | — | 48 |
| 2 | Complete one third | 48 ÷ 3 = 16 completed; 48 − 16 = 32 | 32 |
| 3 | Receive 7 more | 32 + 7 = 39 | 39 |
| 4 | Complete 9 | 39 − 9 = 30 | 30 |

## Final Remaining Job Count

**30 jobs remain.**
