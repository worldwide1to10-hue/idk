**Puzzle 2 Solution**

Step-by-step:

1. **Total items in first batch:** 5 boxes × 12 items each = **60 items**
2. **Items rejected:** 4 items rejected from every box × 5 boxes = **20 rejected items**
3. **Accepted items before transfer:** 60 − 20 = **40 accepted items**
4. **Items moved to second batch:** **7 accepted items** moved out
5. **Accepted items remaining in first batch:** 40 − 7 = **33**

**Answer: 33 accepted items remain in the first batch.**
