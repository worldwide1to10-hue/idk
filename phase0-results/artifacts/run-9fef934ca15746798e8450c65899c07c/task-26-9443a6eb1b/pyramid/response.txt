Puzzle 3: Three jobs take 8, 13, and 5 minutes. The 13-minute job starts after the 8-minute job; the 5-minute job runs in parallel with both.

**Dependency**
- Job B (13 min) depends on Job A (8 min): it cannot start until Job A finishes.
- Job C (5 min) has no dependencies and runs concurrently with both A and B.

**Parallel schedule**
- **t = 0 → 8 min:** Job A runs. Job C runs in parallel during this window and finishes at t = 5 min.
- **t = 8 → 21 min:** Job B runs (started as soon as Job A completed). Job C is already done.

**Critical path:** Job A → Job B = 8 + 13 = 21 minutes. Job C completes at 5 minutes, well before.

**Earliest completion time: 21 minutes**

This is a single straightforward reasoning case (well under eight steps, with a sequential dependency and no three independent branches), so it was completed directly without invoking the Task tool.
