**Puzzle 3 – “Three jobs take 8, 13, and 5 minutes.”**

### Dependency & parallel schedule
| Job | Length | Dependencies | Parallelism |
|-----|--------|--------------|-------------|
| **Job A** | 8 min | – (starts at time 0) | – |
| **Job B** | 13 min | **must start after Job A finishes** (the 13‑minute job cannot begin until the 8‑minute job is complete) | – |
| **Job C** | 5 min | – (independent) | runs **in parallel with both** A and B – it can start at time 0 and continue while A and B are executing |

### How the schedule works
1. **Time 0 – 8 min:**  
   - Job A runs.  
   - Job C also starts at time 0 and runs for its 5 min, finishing at **5 min**.

2. **Time 8 – 21 min:**  
   - Job A has completed, so Job B may now start (it needs the full 13 min, ending at **21 min**).  
   - Job C is already finished, but even if it were allowed to overlap, it would simply be idle after 5 min.

3. **Overall finish:**  
   - Job B is the last job to complete, at **21 min**.  
   - Job C finishes much earlier (5 min) and does not extend the critical path.

### Earliest completion time
Because the only precedence constraint is **A → B**, and the 5‑minute job C can run in parallel without delaying A or B, the whole set of jobs finishes when B ends.

**Earliest completion time = 21 minutes.**
