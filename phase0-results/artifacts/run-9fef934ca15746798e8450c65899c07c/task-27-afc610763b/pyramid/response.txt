**Puzzle 4:** "A library has 120 books. One quarter are fiction. Of the non-fiction books, one third are checked out. How many non-fiction books are available?"

### Step-by-step fraction steps:

- Fiction fraction = 1/4
- Fiction books = 1/4 × 120 = 30

**Step 2 — Find the non-fiction count (fraction form):**
- Non-fiction fraction = 1 − 1/4 = 3/4
- Non-fiction books = 3/4 × 120 = 90
  (Equivalently: 120 − 30 = 90)

**Step 3 — Find how many non-fiction books are checked out:**
- Checked-out fraction of non-fiction = 1/3
- Checked out = 1/3 × 90 = 30

**Step 4 — Find available non-fiction books:**
- Available fraction of non-fiction = 1 − 1/3 = 2/3
- Available = 2/3 × 90 = 60
  (Equivalently: 90 − 30 = 60)

### Answer:
**60 non-fiction books are available.**
