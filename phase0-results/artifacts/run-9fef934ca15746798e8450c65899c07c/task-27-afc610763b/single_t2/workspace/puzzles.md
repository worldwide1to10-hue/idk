# Sequential Reasoning Cases

1. A service starts with 48 queued jobs. It completes one third, then receives 7 more, then completes 9. How many remain?
2. A batch has 5 boxes with 12 items each. Four items are rejected from every box and 7 accepted items are moved to a second batch. How many accepted items remain in the first batch?
3. Three jobs take 8, 13, and 5 minutes. The 13-minute job starts after the 8-minute job; the 5-minute job runs in parallel with both. What is the earliest completion time?
4. A library has 120 books. One quarter are fiction. Of the non-fiction books, one third are checked out. How many non-fiction books are available?
5. A worker processes 18 records per minute for 7 minutes, pauses for 4 minutes, then processes 11 per minute for 3 minutes. How many records are processed?
6. A rectangular table has 9 rows and 14 columns. Two complete columns are removed, then 11 cells are reserved. How many unreserved cells remain?
7. Four contributors complete 6, 8, 11, and 15 reviews. Three reviews fail validation and are repeated once by the same contributors. How many valid reviews are recorded after retries?
