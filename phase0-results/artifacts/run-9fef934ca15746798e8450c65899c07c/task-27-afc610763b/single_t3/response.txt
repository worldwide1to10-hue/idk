**Solution to Puzzle 4**

A library has 120 books. One quarter are fiction. Of the non-fiction books, one third are checked out. How many non-fiction books are available?

**Step-by-step fraction computation:**

1. **Fiction books:** One quarter of the total are fiction.
   $$1/4 \times 120 = 30 \text{ fiction books}$$

2. **Non-fiction books:** The remaining books are non-fiction.
   $$120 - 30 = 90 \text{ non-fiction books}$$
   (Equivalently: $3/4 \times 120 = 90$)

3. **Checked-out non-fiction books:** One third of the non-fiction books are checked out.
   $$1/3 \times 90 = 30 \text{ non-fiction books checked out}$$

4. **Available non-fiction books:** Subtract the checked-out books from the total non-fiction.
   $$90 - 30 = 60 \text{ non-fiction books available}$$
   (Equivalently: if $1/3$ are checked out, $2/3$ are available, so $2/3 \times 90 = 60$)

**Answer: 60 non-fiction books are available.**
