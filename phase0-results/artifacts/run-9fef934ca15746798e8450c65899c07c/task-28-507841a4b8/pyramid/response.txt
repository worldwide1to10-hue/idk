Puzzle 5 states: *"A worker processes 18 records per minute for 7 minutes, pauses for 4 minutes, then processes 11 per minute for 3 minutes. How many records are processed?"*

Calculation:
- First burst: 18 records/min × 7 min = **126 records**
- Pause: 4 minutes → **0 records processed** (pauses contribute nothing)
- Second burst: 11 records/min × 3 min = **33 records**

Total processed records: 126 + 33 = **159 records**
