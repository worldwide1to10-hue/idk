**Puzzle 6 – “A rectangular table has 9 rows and 14 columns. Two complete columns are removed, then 11 cells are reserved. How many unreserved cells remain?”**

1. **Initial number of cells**  
   \[
   9 \text{ rows} \times 14 \text{ columns} = 126 \text{ cells}
   \]

2. **Remove two complete columns**  
   Each column has 9 cells, so removing 2 columns removes  
   \[
   2 \times 9 = 18 \text{ cells}
   \]  
   Remaining cells:  
   \[
   126 - 18 = 108 \text{ cells}
   \]

3. **Reserve 11 cells**  
   \[
   108 - 11 = 97 \text{ cells}
   \]

**Result:** After removing the two columns and reserving 11 cells, **97 unreserved cells remain**.
