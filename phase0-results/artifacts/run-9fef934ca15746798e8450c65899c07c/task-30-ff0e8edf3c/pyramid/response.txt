Puzzle 7 reads: *"Four contributors complete 6, 8, 11, and 15 reviews. Three reviews fail validation and are repeated once by the same contributors. How many valid reviews are recorded after retries?"*

**Step 1 — Count initially completed reviews:**
6 + 8 + 11 + 15 = 40 reviews completed

**Step 2 — Account for validation failures:**
3 reviews fail validation → 40 − 3 = 37 valid reviews initially recorded

**Step 3 — Account for retries (all three retries pass):**
3 retries × 1 pass each → 37 + 3 = 40 valid reviews recorded

**Answer: 40 valid reviews**

All originally completed reviews eventually pass validation (either on first attempt or after retry), so the total valid reviews recorded equals the initial total of 40.
